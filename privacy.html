Privacy Policy — Pixel Runner

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This privacy policy describes what data is collected while using the Pixel Runner ("App", "Game") mobile game for Android.

1. Data stored locally on your device

The App saves the following information on your device (in local browser/WebView storage, not on a server), needed for the game to function:

  • game progress (high score, coin balance, unlocked skins/maps/achievements)
  • sound settings (effects and music volume)
  • a local leaderboard of your best scores on this device

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or to any server — the App has no server or database of its own. Uninstalling the App also removes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob to help fund continued development. AdMob may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier, approximate location, and ad-interaction data — in order to serve ads (including personalized ads, unless you have opted out) and measure their performance.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized advertising data is processed, and can change or withdraw that choice at any time from the App's privacy settings.

Details on what data Google collects and how it is used are available in Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy and Google's advertising policies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. Accounts and sign-in

The App currently does not require creating an account or signing in. If Google sign-in (Google Play Games — cloud progress save, global leaderboard) is added in the future, this policy will be updated in advance, and the feature will be fully optional.

4. Children

The App is not intentionally directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads shown in the App are configured to meet Google's requirements for general-audience content.

5. Your rights

Because the App does not collect or store personal data on any server, there is no data for us to grant access to, correct, or delete on your behalf — all game data lives solely on your device and can be removed at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in Android Settings.
